Making the Digital World Accessible for Everyone

Welcome to the Accessibility Guide This page will help you understand how digital accessibility makes websites and technology easier to use for everyone. People have different abilities and ways of using technology. Some may have difficulty seeing, hearing, moving, or understanding information. Through accessible design and helpful features, we can make digital experiences easier and more inclusive
