Welcome to Accessibility Guide

Digital accessibility means making websites, apps, and online information usable by people with different abilities. People do not all browse the internet in the same way. Some use screen readers, captions, keyboards, voice controls, or simpler page layouts. This guide introduces four areas of accessibility. Choose a topic to learn about common barriers and practical ways to reduce them.

Visual Accessibility Guide

Making text, images, and page layouts easier to understand for people with different visual needs.

Hearing Accessibility Guide

Providing captions, transcripts, and visual information so important content does not depend on sound.

Physical Accessibility Guide

Supporting keyboard navigation and different ways of interacting with a website.

Cognitive Accessibility Guide

Using clear language, familiar layouts, and simple instructions to reduce confusion.
